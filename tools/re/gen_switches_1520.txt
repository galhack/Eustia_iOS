size limits and its proportional text needs the 1.529 metrics).  The side
column is what the condition evaluates to for the 1.520 profile.

tools/re/opset_1520.txt and GEN_1_520 is ordered after GEN_1_529 in
EngineGen_t, so every handler registered from <=1.529 is live and none from
>=1.535 is.
